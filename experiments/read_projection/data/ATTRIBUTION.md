The bAbI QA1 examples come from Meta's English 10k dataset by Weston and colleagues, distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Here two original prefixes with different published answers to the same question form a pair. An additional question asks about a person whose latest location agrees across prefixes. The development split uses training stories and the locked test split uses official test stories. [Dataset and license](https://huggingface.co/datasets/facebook/babi_qa) · [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.05698)

The MQuAKE examples derive from MQuAKE-CF-3k-v2, by Zhong, Wu, Manning and Chen. The two prompts use the old and updated facts. Each pair has the first official multi-hop question and a question from a different case. The included upstream MIT notice applies to these materials.
