Compression, reuse and recomputation have established serving baselines. CacheGen streams compressed Transformer KV caches and can choose recomputation as available bandwidth changes. This experiment charges the changed KV suffix as well as the recurrent and convolution states of a hybrid model. It includes text-patch replay under the same link condition. [CacheGen](https://doi.org/10.1145/3651890.3672274)

Prior low-rank reconstruction methods optimize activation error. QERA derives an activation-dependent reconstruction for quantized weights. In the shared-document pilot the object being sent is a runtime state change, and the candidate basis is estimated from a known template boundary on training documents. The broad ideas of calibration and low-rank approximation are established. [QERA](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/21718991f6acf19a42376b5c7a8668c5-Abstract-Conference.html)
